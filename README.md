# Soc Ops

> **Turn small talk into a game.** Soc Ops is a fast, social bingo experience for in-person mixers where players roam the room, find people who match each prompt, and race to complete **5 in a row**.

Build a playful event experience **and** learn how to shape it with **GitHub Copilot agent-mode**.

## ✨ Why Soc Ops?

Soc Ops is a workshop project that mixes **real-world product thinking** with **AI-assisted development**:

- **For players:** a lightweight social icebreaker that gets everyone moving, talking, and laughing.
- **For developers:** a hands-on lab for building with **FastAPI, Jinja2, HTMX, and Pydantic**.
- **For workshop attendees:** a practical way to explore **GitHub Copilot agent-mode**, prompt design, multi-agent workflows, and design-first iteration.

> Think of it as a tiny game with surprisingly rich lessons in product design, backend structure, frontend UX, and AI collaboration.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Start a game** and share the board with players at your in-person mixer.
2. Each square contains a social prompt, such as finding someone who matches a fun fact, experience, or preference.
3. Players **talk to real people in the room** and mark squares when they find a match.
4. The goal is to complete **5 in a row** — across, down, or diagonal — before everyone else.
5. The result: an easy icebreaker that turns introductions into a playful mission.
